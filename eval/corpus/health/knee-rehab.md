# Physiotherapy review

The physiotherapist reviewed the joint on Tuesday. She expects it to bear
weight normally in six weeks, and to take load on stairs a fortnight before
that. No impact work before then.

The exercises are three sets of fifteen, twice a day: heel slides, straight
leg raises, and the wall squat held for thirty seconds.

Swelling after exercise is expected. Swelling that is still there the next
morning means the session was too much.
