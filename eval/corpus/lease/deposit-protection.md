# Deposit protection

The deposit of 1,675 is held by the Tenancy Deposit Scheme under certificate
TDS-88104-2024. Protection was confirmed on 14 January 2024, within the thirty
day statutory window.

## Making a claim
At the end of the tenancy either side may raise a dispute with the scheme.
The scheme adjudicates from the inventory, the check-out report and any
photographs. Deductions for fair wear and tear are not allowed.

## Prescribed information
The prescribed information was served with the certificate. Without it the
landlord cannot serve a section 21 notice, so keep the copy.
