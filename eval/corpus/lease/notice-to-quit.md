# Ending the tenancy early

To end the agreement before the fixed term expires, serve written notice on
the landlord at the managing agent's address. Two months is the minimum
period. Notice served by email alone is not accepted; post it, and keep the
certificate of posting.

If notice is served in the last two months of the fixed term the agreement
does not renew and the tenancy ends on the last day of the term.

A surrender by agreement is possible at any time but the landlord is not
obliged to accept one, and will usually charge the re-letting cost.
