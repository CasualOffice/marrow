# Assured shorthold tenancy

## Parties and property
This agreement is between Halcyon Property Services (the landlord) and the
occupier of Flat 4, 18 Wentworth Row. It takes effect on 1 January 2024.

## Term and renewal
The fixed term runs for twelve months. At the end of the fixed term the
agreement renews on 31 December for a further twelve months unless either
party gives two months written notice. No new signature is required for the
renewal to take effect; the tenancy simply rolls over on the same terms.

## Rent
Rent of 1,450 a month is payable on the first working day of each month by
standing order. The landlord may propose one increase per twelve month period,
in writing, with two months notice.

## Deposit
A deposit of 1,675 is held. It is registered with a government approved
scheme within thirty days of receipt; see the separate deposit sheet for the
scheme name and the certificate number.

## Repairs
The landlord is responsible for the structure, the heating system and the hot
water. The occupier is responsible for internal decoration and for replacing
light bulbs and smoke alarm batteries.
