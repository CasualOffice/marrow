# Focaccia

Five hundred grams of strong flour, four hundred of water, ten of salt, four
of instant yeast. The dough is wet and should be handled with a wet hand.

Fold in the bowl four times at half hour intervals, then cold prove overnight.
Dimple with oiled fingers, scatter salt and rosemary, and bake as hot as the
oven will go for twenty minutes.

Olive oil in the tin, not just on top. That is what fries the base.
