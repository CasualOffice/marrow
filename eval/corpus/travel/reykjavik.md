# The trip

Five nights in March. The guesthouse is on a quiet street ten minutes from
the harbour and breakfast is included until nine.

The northern lights forecast is published each afternoon; anything above a
three is worth the drive out to the peninsula. Take the coast road, not the
tunnel.

The geothermal pool on the hill is open late and costs almost nothing. The
lagoon everyone photographs is an hour away and needs booking weeks ahead.
