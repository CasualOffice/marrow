# Packing for cold weather

Wool base layer, two pairs. Fleece mid layer. A shell that is genuinely
windproof matters more than one that is warm, because the wind is what makes
it unbearable.

Boots with an aggressive sole for ice. Microspikes if the forecast is near
freezing overnight and above it during the day, which is the worst case.

Swimming things, always. Every town has a heated pool.
